## Primary sources and limits

[Narvar Shield](https://corp.narvar.com/return-exchange) describes return policies, exchanges, return workflows, insights and integrations. Its proprietary fraud intelligence and carrier network are outside this build. No numeric software quote was found on that product page. The public comparison uses words, no price figure. [Narvar overview](https://support.narvar.com/hc/en-us/articles/11307520101651-Narvar-Return-Overview) describes retailer policy and return-reason work.

The internal desk is useful on the business's own records without network access: receiving, inspection, disposition and remedy evidence. It must not be sold as a reproduction of Narvar's network, fraud model or complete shopper experience. A full replacement needs a separately scoped, tested implementation. No claim of exclusive analytical capability or guaranteed savings is made.

The importer reads saved JSON in the published standard RMA webhook format. Merchant archives and historical access are not guaranteed by the public specification. Source structure and limits are documented in the replace guide. All fixtures, demo amounts and command outputs are synthetic. Consumer-rights sources and control mappings are in compliance.md.
